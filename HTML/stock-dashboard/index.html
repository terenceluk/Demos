How to use
💲 Price & currency

The large number is the latest market price. The small code next to it (e.g. USD, CAD) is the trading currency. Hover the chart to see the exact price at any point in time.

📊 52-week bar (L & H)

The thin bar below the price shows where today's price sits within the last 52 weeks. L $xxx is the yearly low (left end), H $xxx is the yearly high (right end). The blue dot marks today's price. Hover the bar for a full summary.

▲▼ Change arrow

Green ▲ means the price is up vs. the previous close; red ▼ means it's down. The dollar and percentage shown are the size of that move. For non-1D ranges the change is from the first to the last data point in the selected window.

📅 Range tabs — 1D · 5D · 1M · 6M · YTD · 1Y · 5Y · Max

Switch the chart window for that card. 1D shows today's intraday price in 2-minute snapshots. Wider ranges use daily or weekly data points. Your choice is remembered per stock across reloads.

🔍 Custom date range

Click Custom on any card, pick a From and To date, then hit Apply. The chart redraws for exactly that window and the panel collapses to a tidy summary. Click Edit to change the dates, or click Custom again to close the panel and go back to a preset tab.

🧾 Shares & P&L

Type how many shares you hold in the Shares field. The dashboard shows your total position value (shares × current price) and today's gain/loss (shares × today's price change). Saved automatically.

☰ Drag to reorder

Grab the ⠿ dots icon in the top-right of any card and drag it on top of another card to swap their order. The new order is saved automatically.

⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts

/ — focus the symbol search box
R — refresh all cards now
(Both are disabled while you're typing in a field.)

➕ Adding & removing stocks

Type a ticker (e.g. AAPL, BTC-USD, BMO.TO) in the search box and pick from the live suggestions, or press Enter. Invalid tickers are rejected with an error message. Hit ✕ on a card to remove it.
